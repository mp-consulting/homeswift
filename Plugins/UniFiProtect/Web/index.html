UniFi Protect

Manage your UniFi Protect cameras and devices in HomeKit.

Controllers

Find a camera's URLs

A third-party camera adopted in Protect streams from its own URLs. Homeswift asks the camera for them over ONVIF; put the ones you pick in the camera's Settings.

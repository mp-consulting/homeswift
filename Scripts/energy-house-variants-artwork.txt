House artwork — Beach house, Woodland cabin and Rooftop apartment

Created with the built-in image generation tool and encoded as WebP. The app renders the animated energy wiring and a shared, precisely spaced solar-module grid over the artwork. The grid uses identical module sizes and gaps within each roof's perspective, and identical geometry across that style's day/night and battery/no-battery variants. Appearance uses the same grid.

Installed files (relative to the Homeswift repository):
Sources/HomeswiftUI/Web/energy-house-{beach,cabin,apartment}.webp
Sources/HomeswiftUI/Web/energy-house-{beach,cabin,apartment}-night.webp
Sources/HomeswiftUI/Web/energy-house-{beach,cabin,apartment}-no-battery.webp
Sources/HomeswiftUI/Web/energy-house-{beach,cabin,apartment}-no-battery-night.webp
Each style also has .json wiring layouts, with and without the battery.

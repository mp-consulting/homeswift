# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, and keep to the work. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and
publishing other people's private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, reviews or anywhere the
project is represented.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through [@mp-consulting](https://github.com/mp-consulting) on
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainers may edit or remove comments and code, and warn, suspend or ban
anyone who does not follow this code, as the Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
